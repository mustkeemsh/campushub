verified_user Strict Admission Route Isolation Guarantee

CollegeHub strictly isolates First-Year engineering cutoffs and never uses First-Year CAP cutoffs to predict Direct Second Year admissions. Polytechnic lateral entry cutoffs, vacancy seat matrices, and diploma reservation quotas operate under independent rules and cannot be evaluated with First-Year data.

checklist Required DSY Cutoff Data to Provide:
  • State CET Cell DSE CAP Round 1, 2, or 3 Allotment Cutoff Matrix (PDF / CSV)
  • Official fields: Institute Code, Course Code, Degree Branch, Category (GOPEN, GOBC, etc.), Quota (Home Univ HU / Other Than Home OHU / State Level SL)
  • Cutoff metrics: Closing State General Merit Number (Merit Rank) or Diploma Aggregate %
